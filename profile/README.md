# Building more predictable, skill-based hiring

[selectry](https://www.selectry.io) is a Berlin-based HR technology company. We combine structured recruiting workflows, intelligent automation, and human recruiting expertise to help hiring teams reach better candidate decisions faster—without losing control of the process.

## What we build

- Structured, repeatable recruiting pipelines
- Skill-based candidate matching and decision support
- AI-assisted workflows with human oversight
- Practical tools for recruiters and hiring teams

Our product is currently in private beta. Most product development happens in private repositories; selected engineering resources may be published here when they are ready for public use.

## How we work

We value clear ownership, thoughtful review, secure-by-default engineering, and respectful collaboration. Changes to our repositories are reviewed before release, and security concerns are handled through private reporting channels.
