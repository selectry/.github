# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and professional boundaries.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the relevant repository administrators or through an established internal company channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inconsistent with this policy.

## Scope

This policy applies in selectry repositories and other spaces where someone is representing selectry or its community.
