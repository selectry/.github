# Support

## Product and business enquiries

Visit [selectry.io](https://www.selectry.io) for product and company information and the current contact options.

## Repository questions

For reproducible bugs or documentation problems, use the repository's issue templates. Include the repository version or commit, expected and actual behavior, reproduction steps, and relevant non-sensitive logs.

Please do not use public issues for account-specific questions, customer or candidate data, credentials, internal operational details, or security vulnerabilities.

## Security

Suspected vulnerabilities must be reported privately as described in our [security policy](SECURITY.md).
