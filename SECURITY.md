# Security policy

Security is a shared responsibility at selectry. We appreciate responsible reports that help us protect our customers, candidates, team, and systems.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do **not** disclose a suspected vulnerability in a public issue, discussion, pull request, or social-media post.

For a public repository that offers **Report a vulnerability** on its Security tab, use that private reporting form. For private repositories and internal systems, contact the repository administrators through an established private company channel and include `SECURITY` in the subject or first line.

Include, where possible:

- the affected repository, service, or component;
- a clear description of the issue and its potential impact;
- reproducible steps or a minimal proof of concept;
- any suggested mitigation; and
- a safe way to contact you for follow-up.

Do not access, modify, retain, or share data that is not yours. Avoid privacy violations, service disruption, social engineering, denial-of-service testing, and automated scanning that could degrade our systems.

## What to expect

We will acknowledge a complete report as soon as reasonably possible, assess severity and scope, and coordinate remediation and disclosure. Please allow us a reasonable opportunity to investigate and fix the issue before publishing details.

## Supported versions

Only actively maintained versions and deployed services are supported. Repository maintainers can clarify whether a specific version remains supported.
